Questions we hear a lot

Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

Avril introduces itself as your virtual receptionist. Callers are told honestly, and most are happy to get an instant answer instead of voicemail.

What happens if Avril can't answer something?

It takes a message, promises a callback and flags the call for you. For urgent matters it transfer to a person on your team.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number and forward calls to Avril, either all the time or only aftr hours and when you're busy.

How does the AI know my business?

We train Avril on your services, prices, hours and policies. You can also add FAQs and special instructions.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses go live within five working days, after one short onboarding call.
